Years of Mission

A credit union's act of faith became something bigger.

In 2007, Fidelis Catholic Credit Union founded the Fidelis Foundation as its own independent nonprofit — because they believed that supporting Catholic education deserved a dedicated home, not just a line item. Nearly twenty years later, we've helped families across the Archdiocese of Denver make Catholic high school a reality. As we approach our twentieth anniversary, we're building toward something even larger.

Our Programs

Making Catholic high school
more accessible.

Tuition costs shouldn't be the reason a family says no to a Catholic education. The Fidelis Foundation works to close that gap — for students and for the teachers who pour into them every day.

I

Student Scholarships

We award scholarships to eighth-grade students in the Archdiocese of Denver who are pursuing a Catholic high school education — helping more families make it happen. Apply for a scholarship →

II

Teacher Grants

Catholic school teachers give generously of themselves every day. We support them with salary assistance grants and professional development funding — including grants for teachers incorporating financial literacy into their curriculum.

III

Financial Literacy

Rooted in the Catholic understanding of stewardship, we work to bring real financial education into Catholic high schools across the Archdiocese — giving students tools to live generously and wisely for life.

Our Focus

We exist to make Catholic high school more affordable in Denver.

The cost of Catholic education is real — and it keeps some families from even considering it. The Fidelis Foundation is here to change that, specifically at the high school level in the Archdiocese of Denver. Our scholarships, teacher support, and financial literacy work all aim at the same goal: making Catholic high school a realistic option for more families.
